# Privacy Policy — TheNewsGuyBot / [@thenewsguybot](https://www.instagram.com/thenewsguybot/)

**Last updated:** 2025-11-28

## 1. Introduction
TheNewsGuyBot (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Instagram account **@thenewsguybot**, which automatically posts news updates every three hours.  
This Privacy Policy explains how the Service handles data while fetching news from external sources and posting them to Instagram.

## 2. No User Data Collection
We do **not** collect, store, process, analyze, or track any personal data from Instagram users.

- We do not store usernames, messages, comments, or profile information.  
- We do not track user behavior or interactions.  
- We do not run analytics on Instagram user activity.  
- We do not use cookies or tracking of any sort.

The bot interacts only with our own Instagram account and does not process any user-related information.

## 3. News Sources and Content Collection
TheNewsGuyBot automatically retrieves news from **publicly available third-party sources**, such as:

- News APIs  
- Public RSS feeds  
- Public news websites  
- Authorized content providers  

### What we collect from these sources:
- News headlines  
- Article summaries or excerpts  
- Publicly available metadata (e.g., timestamps, categories, authors — if provided publicly)

### What we **do not** collect:
- Any personal data of individuals from these news sources  
- Any user-specific or identifiable data  

All fetched news content is used **only for generating posts on @thenewsguybot**.

## 4. Instagram API Usage
The Service uses the Instagram Graph API **only** for:

- Publishing scheduled posts to our own Instagram Business Account (@thenewsguybot)  
- Managing media uploaded by the bot  

The bot does **not** read or store data from any other Instagram users.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure
Since we do not collect personal user data:

- No user data is shared or sold  
- No user data is transferred to third parties  

The only external communication is fetching news content from public news providers.

## 6. Data Retention
Because we do not collect user data, no personal data is retained.  
Temporary logs (for debugging) may be generated by servers but contain **no personal information** and are deleted automatically.

## 7. Security
We take reasonable steps to secure our systems and API keys.  
Since we do not store personal data, exposure risk to users is zero.

## 8. Children’s Privacy
The Service is not intended for children under 13.  
We do not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

## 9. Third-Party Content and Links
TheNewsGuyBot posts news summaries with links to third-party websites.  
These external sites have their own privacy policies and practices.  
We do not control their data handling, and users should review those policies separately.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy if our functionality changes.  
The “Last updated” date reflects the latest revision.
